02 / Primary research · 20 user interviews

Four patterns that shaped every design decision

“I Googled my daughter’s fever at 2am and got 6 different answers. I just needed one trusted person to tell me what to do.”
Insight 01

“Parents got conflicting advice from 40+ sources in their baby’s first 6 months.”

→ Information overload was the reported pain. Parents couldn’t evaluate which advice to trust.

Insight 02

“Paediatricians average 12 minutes per visit. Parents have questions at 3am.”

→ The expert-access gap was critical: no trusted resource outside scheduled appointments.

Insight 03

“Information needs change every 2–4 weeks in infancy. Generic apps feel irrelevant fast.”

→ Stage-specific content was the differentiator parents wanted most.

Insight 04

“70% trusted other parents more than websites, but feared bad advice from unverified sources.”

→ Community was valued, but trust and verification were the barrier to engagement.
